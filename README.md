## Key sources in context

Background for the quantum deep-learning writing linked above, and for the notebook in this repository.

- **B19.** Broughton, Michael; Verdon, Guillaume; McCourt, Trevor; et al. "TensorFlow Quantum: A Software Framework for Quantum Machine Learning." arXiv:2003.02989, 2020. [arxiv.org/abs/2003.02989](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.02989)  
  *In context:* `lang_c.ipynb` is TensorFlow's "Install TensorFlow for C" guide; TFQ is the quantum machine-learning layer built on the same TensorFlow framework.
- **B26.** Biamonte, Jacob; Wittek, Peter; Pancotti, Nicola; Rebentrost, Patrick; Wiebe, Nathan; Lloyd, Seth. "Quantum machine learning." *Nature* 549(7671): 195–202, 2017. DOI: [10.1038/nature23474](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature23474)  
  *In context:* a map of the quantum machine-learning field the blog writes about, including caveats such as data loading.
- **B24.** Farhi, Edward; Neven, Hartmut. "Classification with Quantum Neural Networks on Near Term Processors." arXiv:1802.06002, 2018. [arxiv.org/abs/1802.06002](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.06002)  
  *In context:* the quantum-neural-network idea at the center of "quantum deep learning".
- **B27.** McClean, Jarrod R.; Boixo, Sergio; Smelyanskiy, Vadim N.; Babbush, Ryan; Neven, Hartmut. "Barren plateaus in quantum neural network training landscapes." *Nature Communications* 9: 4812, 2018. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-018-07090-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-07090-4)  
  *In context:* the key counterweight to optimistic claims about training quantum neural networks.
- **B23.** Preskill, John. "Quantum Computing in the NISQ era and beyond." *Quantum* 2: 79, 2018. DOI: [10.22331/q-2018-08-06-79](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2018-08-06-79)  
  *In context:* the framing for what today's noisy devices can and cannot do, which keeps the blog's claims measured.

The B-numbers (B19, B23, B24, B26, B27) refer to the full annotated bibliography in the private [merwin-flagship-projects](https://github.com/davidmerwin/merwin-flagship-projects/blob/main/bibliography/ANNOTATED_BIBLIOGRAPHY.md) repository, which requires access. Each source is cited for background and method; none is used to claim a quantum advantage.
